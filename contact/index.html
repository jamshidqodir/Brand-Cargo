Frequently asked questions

Got questions? We’ve answered some of the most common ones.

What services does Brand Cargo offer?

We provide Amazon Relay and Freight Partner trucking, along with full truckload (FTL) and OTR/street freight services across all 48 contiguous states.

Can I track my shipment in real-time?

Yes. Every load is tracked with real-time GPS, so you always know exactly where your shipment is throughout its journey.

Do you haul Amazon Relay loads?

Yes, Amazon Relay and Freight Partner hauls are one of our core services. We consistently deliver on-time performance for scheduled Amazon loads.

How secure are my goods during transport?

All shipments are handled by experienced, vetted drivers using proper loading and securing procedures, with full insurance coverage on every load.

What is the estimated delivery time?

Delivery times depend on route and distance, but with our nationwide coverage and 98% on-time rate, most loads arrive on schedule as planned.

Can I change or cancel a shipment after booking?

Yes, contact our dispatch team as early as possible — changes and cancellations are handled case by case depending on how close the load is to pickup.
